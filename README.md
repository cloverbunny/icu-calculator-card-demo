# ICU Bedside Calculator (teaching demo)

> ⚠ **Teaching demo — not for clinical use.** Do not use these numbers for patient care.

A single-page calculator with no dependencies. Everything runs in the browser, and no data is saved or sent anywhere.

1. **Lung-protective tidal volume:** ARDSNet predicted body weight and Vt at 4, 6 and 8 mL/kg PBW (height in cm, inches, or feet + inches)
2. **P/F ratio:** PaO₂ / FiO₂ with the Berlin ARDS oxygenation category (FiO₂ entered as 50 or 0.5)
3. **Mean arterial pressure:** from SBP and DBP, flagged when below 65 mmHg
4. **Infusion rate:** mcg/kg/min or mcg/min to mL/hr

To use it offline, download `index.html` and double-click it.
