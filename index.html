1 · Lung-protective tidal volume

ARDSNet predicted body weight (PBW) and Vt at 4, 6 and 8 mL/kg PBW

PBW (kg): male = 50 + 0.91 × (height cm − 152.4); female = 45.5 + 0.91 × (height cm − 152.4).

2 · P/F ratio

PaO₂ / FiO₂ with Berlin ARDS oxygenation category

Berlin categories (on PEEP or CPAP ≥ 5 cmH₂O): mild 200–300 · moderate 100–200 · severe ≤ 100. The ratio alone does not diagnose ARDS. Berlin also needs onset within 1 week, bilateral opacities, and edema not fully explained by cardiac failure or fluid overload.

3 · Mean arterial pressure

MAP ≈ DBP + ⅓ (SBP − DBP)

This is an estimate. An arterial line reports MAP directly, and that value is more accurate.

4 · Infusion rate

Converts a dose to a pump rate in mL/hr

Rate (mL/hr) = dose (mcg/min) × 60 ÷ concentration (mcg/mL). For mcg/kg/min the dose is first multiplied by weight. Example: 4 mg norepinephrine in 250 mL = 16 mcg/mL.

Teaching demo only. Formulas: ARDSNet (NEJM 2000) PBW; Berlin Definition (JAMA 2012); standard MAP estimate; standard weight-based infusion arithmetic.
